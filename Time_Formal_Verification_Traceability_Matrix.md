## 1. Purpose

This matrix provides bidirectional traceability between the conceptual statements in the Time derivation and their manuscript-facing Lean interfaces.

As in the SBI verification matrix, each row separates four questions:

1. **Identification:** Is the conceptual statement uniquely identified and sourced?
2. **Formal representation:** Is there a specific Lean declaration whose statement or type represents the conceptual claim?
3. **Semantic fidelity:** Does the Lean declaration express the same claim, or is it stronger, weaker, indirect, conditional, or dependent on an explicit semantic encoding?
4. **Formal verification:** Does the claimed result follow in Lean from the stated formal inputs, with the project building successfully?

Lean certifies deductive correctness of the encoded statements. It does not establish the physical truth of the imported SBI framework, the physical applicability of the record-forming conditions, the local duration calibration, or the coarse-grained effective assumptions. Those logical-status distinctions are therefore retained explicitly in the matrix.

## 2. NASA-style tailoring

This matrix follows the same tailored Requirements Verification Matrix approach used for the SBI formalization. The standard NASA verification vocabulary is retained, while hardware-specific fields are replaced by scientific-formalization fields: Lean interface, source file, conceptual inputs, semantic-fidelity criterion, verification method, and evidence/result.

### Verification-method codes

- **I — Inspection:** direct comparison of the conceptual statement with the Lean declaration and its comments/type.
- **A — Analysis:** examination of the Lean proof, dependency structure, quotient construction, or logical reduction to supporting declarations.
- **T — Test:** successful clean `lake build` / elaboration of the checked Lean project.
- **D — Demonstration:** reserved for executable demonstrations if introduced later.

Most theorem rows use **I+A+T**. Pure definitions, conditions, and semantic contracts normally use **I+T**.

### Result/status codes

- **PASS** — conceptual statement and manuscript-facing Lean interface agree at the level relevant to the derivation.
- **PASS+** — Lean establishes the manuscript claim and additionally proves a stronger or more explicit result; the strengthening is documented.
- **ENCODING** — the row is a semantic/representational contract or deliberate representation choice rather than a derived physical law.
- **EXPLANATORY** — scope or interpretation statement for which no dedicated Lean theorem is required.
- **UPDATE** — Lean is internally coherent but manuscript wording or dependency statements require alignment.
